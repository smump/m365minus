How the count works

Every year starts at 365 (366 in a leap year). Each major Microsoft 365 or Azure outage felt across North America — the front-page-of-r/sysadmin kind — subtracts one day. Four qualifying outages and the counter reads M361.

The bar is deliberately high. A niche service blipping for ten minutes doesn't count. "Everyone's refreshing the status page and can't work" does. Judgment calls (looking at you, CrowdStrike) get logged with counts:false — on the record, but no day docked.
